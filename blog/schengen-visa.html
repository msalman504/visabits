Applying for a Schengen Visa
From the United Kingdom

Written by Salman • Immigration & Compliance Consultant

Indian, Pakistani, or Nigerian passport holder living in the UK on a BRP? Navigating VFS Global and TLScontact successfully requires meeting highly specific criteria. This guide outlines the exact, non-negotiable rules to secure your approval.

The 3-Month BRP Expiry Threshold

Consulates enforce a strict residency policy: your **UK Biometric Residence Permit (BRP)** or digital immigration status **must be valid for at least 3 months beyond your planned return date** from the Schengen Zone.

If your BRP expires in less than 3 months on the day you leave Europe, TLScontact or VFS Global will reject your application at the front desk. There are no exceptions to this rule.

Standard Mandatory Documentation

Unlike applications submitted from your home country, UK consulates require rigorous evidence of your active ties to the UK. Ensure you have these verified items prepared:

1. 3-Month Financial Statements

You must present your last 3 months of bank statements showing an active, consistent balance of **£3,000 to £4,500**. Regular salary deposits are essential. "Lump-sum dumps" (transferring large sums right before printing statements) will trigger an immediate refusal.

2. Compliant Schengen Travel Insurance

Your policy must specifically state it covers all Schengen States (or Worldwide) with a medical limit of at least **€30,000**, including full repatriation coverage. Free credit card travel insurance is rarely accepted unless accompanied by a formal visa certificate.

3. Proof of Employment or Study

A signed and stamped letter from your employer or university registrar, dated within 30 days of your appointment, confirming your role, start date, salary, and that you are expected back to resume your duties.

Consular & Service Fees

Visa and processing charges consist of the standard embassy fee and the center's handling charge:

Category Standard Cost Notes
Consular Visa Fee (Adults) €90 (~£78) Standard government visa processing fee.
Consular Visa Fee (Kids 6-12) €45 (~£39) Reduced rate for children; under 6 is exempt.
VFS / TLScontact Handling Charge £25 - £40 Administrative appointment slot booking charge.

The Application Pathway

01 /

**Consulate Selection:** Apply to the country where you spend the most nights. If nights are equal, apply to the country that serves as your first port of entry into the Schengen zone.

02 /

**Flight & Hotel Bookings:** Never purchase non-refundable flight tickets before receiving your visa. Utilize verifiable flight reservations and fully cancellable hotel bookings to safeguard your travel finances.

03 /

**Biometrics Submission:** Schedule your appointment at TLScontact or VFS Global. Hand over your passport, give fingerprints, and expect a standard processing time of **10 to 15 calendar days**.

Strategic Warnings

Ensure your bank statements are either officially stamped by the branch or include a digital verification barcode (e.g., Monzo or Revolut PDF statements). Standard home printer copies lacking security elements are often rejected on the spot.

Additionally, make certain every character of your name exactly matches across your passport, BRP, travel insurance certificate, and employer letter. Discrepancies lead to immediate rejections at check-in.
