Living in the UK on an Indian, Pakistani, or Nigerian passport? Securing your Schengen Visa doesn't have to be a guessing game. Below is the precise framework to ensure a successful application at TLScontact or VFS Global.

Critical Residence Expiry Rule

Embassy rules are absolute. Your **UK Biometric Residence Permit (BRP)** or digital status **MUST be valid for at least 3 months (90 days)** after you return from your trip. If it expires sooner, consular centers will reject your application immediately at the desk.

01 Mandatory Document Checklist

Applying for a Schengen Visa from the UK requires specific supporting documents. Missing even a single item can result in immediate rejection or refusal:

Official Signed Proof of Ties

An employment verification letter printed on official company letterhead, signed by HR, and dated within 30 days of your appointment. For students, an official registrar-signed enrollment certificate.

3 Months of Structured Bank Statements

Official stamped bank statements showing regular salary deposits and maintaining a consistent stable balance. Avoid cash deposits or transferring large lump sums right before print dates (commonly flagged as "funds dumping").

Consular-Approved Itinerary & Insurance

Confirmed round-trip flight reservations, hotel vouchers matching your itinerary, and a formal visa-compliant travel insurance policy covering medical limits up to **€30,000** with repatriation.

02 The Application Journey

Follow this sequence strictly to secure your visa without delays:

1

Pre-Application Document Verification

Gather all bank statements, BRP scans, and employment verification letters. Ensure bank statements are official and stamped.

2

Online Portal Completion & Slot Booking

Complete the official online portal questions for the target consulate, upload your files, pay the handling fees, and lock your biometrics slot.

3

Attend Biometrics Appointment

Submit your passport and original documents at the application center (VFS Global / TLScontact). Biometrics capture and passport submission takes **10 to 15 days**.
