Step-by-Step VFS & TLS Guide

Living in the UK on an Indian, Pakistani, or Nigerian passport? Securing your Schengen visa doesn't have to be a guessing game. Below is the precise framework to ensure a successful application at TLScontact or VFS Global.

01 / The Non-Negotiable 3-Month BRP Expiry Rule

Embassy rules are absolute. Your **UK Biometric Residence Permit (BRP)** or digital status **MUST be valid for at least 3 months after you return** from the Schengen Zone.

Immediate Check-In Rejection

If your BRP expires in less than 90 days from your planned departure date, VFS or TLS officials will decline to accept your passport on the spot. Make sure to double-check your dates before booking appointment slots.

02 / Mandatory Document Checklist

Ensure your documents conform to this exact standard. Missing a single stamp or signature can lead to a refusal letter:

3 Months Official Bank Statements

Must show regular salary deposits and maintain a consistent balance above £3,000. Avoid cash deposits or transferring large lump sums right before print dates (commonly flagged as "funds dumping").

Consular-Approved Travel Insurance

A formal visa certificate confirming a minimum medical limit of **€30,000**, zero deductible/excess, and active repatriation coverage across all Schengen member states.

Official Signed Proof of Ties

An employment letter printed on letterhead, signed by HR, and dated within 30 days of the appointment. If studying, an official registrar-signed enrollment letter.

03 / Consular & Appointment Charges

Embassy processing charges are standard, but the third-party appointment handling center fees are separate and non-refundable:

Fee Class Cost (GBP Equivalent) Details
Consular Visa Fee (Adults) €90 (~£78) Embassy standard government visa check charge.
Consular Visa Fee (Ages 6-12) €45 (~£39) Consular fee; children under 6 are completely free.
TLScontact / VFS Handling Fee £25 - £40 Booking center administration charge paid when locking slot.

04 / The Application Journey

1

Determine the Correct Consulate

Apply to the country of your main destination. If spending equal time in multiple countries, apply to the country that serves as your first port of entry.

2

Verify Bookings & Reservations

Gather confirmed hotel vouchers and flight itineraries. Use verifiable, non-payment holds to protect your money prior to final approval.

3

Attend Biometrics Appointment

Submit your printed files at TLScontact or VFS Global, give fingerprints, and submit your passport. Processing takes **10 to 15 days**.
