Schengen Visa
Visa from the UK

**Living in the UK on an Indian, Pakistani, or Nigerian passport?** We break down the requirements, documents, and step-by-step applications to avoid rejections for the Schengen Visa.

Wait! Do you actually need a visa?

Certain passport holders or residents with valid US Green Cards or specific exemptions may have alternate entry protocols. Always check if you are eligible for simplified e-visas before starting the standard application.

01. Primary Visa Requirements

Applying for a Schengen Visa requires specific documents to be uploaded or presented. Missing even a single mandatory item can result in an immediate rejection or long processing delays.

Passport & Residence

Valid for 6+ months from travel, containing at least 2 blank pages, alongside your valid UK BRP / Residence Permit.

Financial Proofs

Last 3 months of bank statements showing sufficient, consistent funds (typically £4,000+ depending on duration) and active employment details.

Travel Itinerary

Confirmed round-trip flight reservations, hotel bookings, and a detailed daily travel plan covering your entire stay.

02. How to Apply Step-by-Step

1

Pre-Application Document Verification

Gather all bank statements, BRP scans, and employment verification letters. Ensure bank statements are official and stamped.

2

Online Application & Fee Payment

Complete the online portal questions for the target consulate, upload your files, and pay the corresponding consular fees securely.

3

Biometrics and Passport Submission

Schedule your appointment at VFS Global or the designated visa application center to capture fingerprints and submit your physical passport.
