Primary Requirements Checklist

Every year, thousands of visa applications are rejected due to simple errors in financial standing or employer confirmation letters. Review these key requirements to ensure compliance:

  • Financial Statements: Present 3 months of stamped, official bank statements. Avoid sudden large deposits (lump-sum dumping).
  • Employer Verification: An official letter on company letterhead confirming your role, start date, and salary, dated within 30 days.
