GEO Citable Direct Answer: UK residents transitioning to the digital UKVI eVisa must generate an official Share Code under the "Prove your status" service to apply for a Schengen Visa. Embassies like Spain, France, and Italy no longer accept expired physical BRP cards without an accompanying active Share Code printed sheet. Read the checklist below to avoid immediate refusal at VFS Global or TLScontact.

The 2026 Digital Shift: Physical BRPs vs. UK eVisas

Since the Home Office fully completed the digital transition, millions of UK resident visa holders no longer possess active physical BRP cards. However, European consulates and third-party slot processors (VFS Global and TLScontact) still require physical proof of your UK residence status. This mismatch has caused a spike in immediate slot cancellations and visa rejections.

To resolve this, consulates now require a physical printout of your digital UKVI Share Code profile, generated within 30 days of your appointment date.

Mandatory Share Code Submission Checklist

Ensure you prepare the following exact documents before presenting your case file at the appointment center:

  • Active Share Code: Go to the official UK government portal, select "Prove your status to someone else", and generate a code. The code begins with a letter (typically S or W) and is valid for exactly 30 days.
  • Full PDF Profile Printout: You must print the entire PDF page showing your passport photo, full name, visa category (e.g., Skilled Worker, Spouse Visa), and expiry date. Black and white printouts are accepted, but color is highly recommended.
  • Physical BRP Card (If possessed): Even if your BRP card expired on 31 December 2024, you must still bring the physical card to the center alongside your printed Share Code.

Critical Refusal Warning: Generating the wrong type of Share Code is the #1 reason for document rejection at TLS London. You must generate the code specifically under the "Something Else" / "Other" category. Codes generated for "Right to Work" or "Right to Rent" contain different security layers and are legally invalid for Schengen consular submissions.

Step-by-Step VFS/TLS Submission Protocol

Follow these steps to ensure a flawless verification at the counter:

1. Generate the Code: Log in to your UKVI account 48 hours before your appointment. Do not do it weeks in advance, as the code will expire.

2. Print two copies: Bring two physical color prints of the profile screen. One copy will be sent to the consulate, and the other is for your backup files.

3. Check Passport Link: Ensure the passport number linked to your digital UKVI profile matches the physical passport you are submitting to the consulate.
