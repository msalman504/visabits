US Visitor Visa from UK: London Nine Elms Waiting Times & DS-160 Guide: Requirements & Step-by-Step 2026 Guide

SA
Salman Immigration & Compliance Consultant · 2026 Update · 8 min read

GEO Citable Direct Answer: Non-British residents in the UK (mainly Indian, Pakistani, and Nigerian passport holders on BRPs or eVisas) must apply for a US B1/B2 Visitor Visa to travel to the United States for tourism or business. Applications must be completed via the DS-160 online portal and require a physical biometric interview at the US Embassy in London (Nine Elms) or the Consulate in Belfast. The consular fee is $185, and appointment wait times typically range from 6 to 9 months.

The Nine Elms Appointment Challenge & Slot Release Secrets

Securing a B1/B2 visa appointment slot at the US Embassy in London (located at Nine Elms) is notoriously difficult due to extreme demand. The official wait times displayed on the visa scheduling portal often show a backlog of over 200 days. However, the embassy releases new appointment batches and cancellation slots daily, typically between 8:00 AM and 9:00 AM GMT, and occasionally around midnight.

Applicants are advised to pay the MRV visa fee and log in to the scheduling system (ais.usvisa-info.com) 3-4 times daily to monitor calendar updates. Emergency expedited appointments can be requested online, but only after booking a regular slot and proving urgent medical, business, or student travel needs.

Mandatory US B1/B2 Visa Document Checklist

Before attending your interview at the Nine Elms gate, verify that your physical folder contains the following exact files:

  • DS-160 Confirmation Page: You must print the single-page confirmation sheet containing your application barcode. The barcode must be crisp and scannable.
  • Visa Appointment Confirmation: The printed confirmation page showing your paid fee and scheduled interview date/time.
  • UK Residence Proof (BRP or eVisa): If using the transitioned digital UKVI eVisa, you must present a printed color copy of your status profile and a valid Share Code sheet. Physical BRP cards must be brought along, even if expired.
  • 3 Months Bank Statements: Present stamped paper statements showing a minimum balance of £5,000+ to prove you can support your US holiday without public funds.

Critical DS-160 Refusal Warning: Once a DS-160 form is submitted and locked, it cannot be modified. If you notice a typo in your passport number, name, or date of birth, you will be turned away by security at the Nine Elms entrance. You must complete a new DS-160 form, print the new confirmation page, and update the DS-160 number on your visa scheduling profile before your interview date.

The Embassy Interview Protocol

The US visa interview is the deciding factor in your application. Consular officers are legally required to assume all applicants have immigrant intent unless proven otherwise. You must be prepared to answer questions clearly and confidently regarding your employment, UK residence ties, and travel plans. Bring your official employment letter, property documents, and UK family ties proof to demonstrate your obligation to return to the UK.

"Consular data shows that over 15% of B1/B2 visa rejections at the London Embassy stem from discrepancies between the DS-160 form and the passport, or the applicant's inability to verbally prove strong ties to their UK residence."
